A local AI assistant for Windows. Work on your code, explore science and the arts, or plan everyday projects with categorized specialist roles. Review edits by default, or enable Auto-Approval for a trusted project. The AI runs on your PC, with no cloud AI account or subscription. Online connections are optional.

Features in development

A preview of our upcoming release. Public downloads are coming soon; connected services still need live account checks before launch.

  • Private, local AI

    The model runs on your PC with llama.cpp, and local coding needs no cloud AI account. Projects, chats and imported notes are stored locally. Optional online features connect only when you enable and use them.

  • Works offline

    After the required model and voice downloads, chat, project tools, tutoring and instruction packs work on your own PC without internet. Downloads, update checks, online research and service connections use the network.

  • Your choice of approval

    Review file changes and commands by default. Approval requests have no countdown and return after a refresh while the app stays open. For a trusted project, turn on Allow Auto-Approval for reversible edits and test/build/check commands. File backups and activity records remain.

  • Project code search

    Open any folder. Offline Coder indexes your code and finds matches with file and line numbers, so answers are grounded in your actual project.

  • Qwen3 models picked by RAM

    It checks your memory and graphics card and recommends Qwen3 8B, Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 or Llama 3.2 3B. Switch models any time.

  • Auto-tune for your PC

    Re-tune for this PC benchmarks your hardware and picks the fastest engine (Vulkan GPU or CPU), context size and threads.

  • Voice input

    Click Voice, speak, click Stop. Speech recognition runs locally with whisper.cpp, so your voice stays on your PC too.

  • Knowledge library import

    Import your official ChatGPT, Grok or Claude export, or a folder of notes, so answers can draw on your own past conversations. Stored locally.

  • Saved conversations

    Chats save on your PC with automatic topic titles. Start a new chat without losing earlier conversations. Search, rename, archive, restore or delete a chat after confirmation.

  • Settings by category

    Open Assistant, Models & speed, Knowledge, Extensions, Connections, Appearance or Updates directly. Switch tabs without losing your input values.

  • 82 specialist roles, organized around your interests

    Leave Auto on for local topic selection, or choose a role yourself. Each role gives your selected local model focused instructions. A targeted second opinion is available when needed; all roles use your selected model.

  • A real agent workflow

    A visible task plan, project notes it remembers between chats, git status/diff/log and commits, a test runner that finds pytest, npm, dotnet, cargo, go or ctest, multi-file patches with one approval, and background commands for dev servers.

  • Extensions by specialty

    Browse coding, education, science, farming, cooking, automotive, engineering, building design, arts, animal care, health and self-reliance packs by category.   • Optional service connections

    The development build includes Gmail, GitHub, GitLab, Notion, Slack, Dropbox, OneDrive, Outlook, Google Drive, Google Calendar local Ghidra Binary Analysis and Local MCP Tools adapters. Ghidra inspection covers functions, strings, reconstructed code, assembly and references in a program you select; Ghidra and the GhidraMCP plugin are installed separately. Local MCP supports one separately installed HTTP server with reviewed tools; every invocation asks approval, including with Auto-Approval. Connections stay off until enabled. Google sign-in needs app registration; other online services currently use encrypted access tokens. Provider permissions and live connection checks are required before release.

  • Online research and email

    Optional research can look up current information and cite sources. Gmail can read mail and prepare drafts; sending still requires your Send button and confirmation, even with project Auto-Approval on. Other mail providers retain advanced IMAP/SMTP setup.

  • Themes and see-through window

    Eleven themes including Midnight blue, Light paper, Terminal green and Amber CRT, or your own colors. Adjustable font size, a text glow slider, and a background opacity slider that lets your desktop show through while text stays crisp.

  • Clearer, more reliable work

    Chat streams locally, with fewer decorative reactions during tasks. Unchanged failed actions are blocked, proposed Python, PowerShell, JavaScript/TypeScript, C#, Java, Rust and Go receive syntax checks, and claims about edits or tests require matching tool evidence. Model answers can still need review.

  • Built-in coding checks

    Specialists are guided to choose fast project search, structural outlines, Python lint/type diagnostics and multi-language parsing without another toggle. Checks help catch mistakes, while focused tests and review still matter.

  • Knowledge search by meaning

    Imported notes build a local meaning-search index automatically in the background, alongside keyword search. Import an image for English OCR. Notes and embeddings stay on your PC; OCR can misread text. The English OCR engine and recognition data are bundled, with no online account or Windows language-pack setup required.

  • More responsive settings

    Background status checks, cached menus and smoother slider and window updates keep interface work lightweight. Local topic routing avoids an extra model call. Reply speed still depends on your model and PC.

  • Sparing reactions

    Add your own reactions. Assistant reactions are occasional in casual conversation and disabled during project tool tasks.

  • Stop button

    Request cancellation of a reply or tool task whenever needed. You stay in control of the assistant's work.

Frequently asked questions

Does Offline Coder really work without the internet?

Yes. After the required model and voice downloads, chat, code search, file edits, tutoring and local instruction packs run on your PC without internet. Downloads, update checks, online research and optional service connections use the network. Optional connections start disabled.

Is my code or chat history sent anywhere?

The AI model runs locally, and projects, chats and imported knowledge are stored on your PC. Online research sends search queries to its provider; enabled extensions communicate with the service you connect. Retrieved content is processed by the local model. Connection credentials are encrypted for your Windows account and are not supplied as credentials to the model. Approved commands run with your Windows account and may use the network.

Is it a private, offline alternative to ChatGPT or Copilot?

For everyday coding help, the development build provides local chat, project search, proposed edits and approved commands. Local inference stays on your PC. Optional research, connected services and commands that use the network can communicate online. Smaller local models can require more steps and review for complex work.

Which AI models does it use?

Open-weight models running on llama.cpp. On first run Offline Coder checks your RAM and graphics card and recommends one: Qwen3 8B with 12 GB of RAM or more (or an 8 GB graphics card), Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 with 8 GB, and Llama 3.2 3B on smaller PCs. You can switch models at any time in Settings.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB of RAM minimum (16 GB recommended) and 3 to 6 GB of free disk space for a model. A graphics card with Vulkan support makes replies much faster, but a CPU works too. Settings > Re-tune for this PC picks the fastest settings for your hardware.

Can the assistant change my files without asking?

By default it previews edits and commands and waits for approval, except commands on your project's allowed list. Approvals have no countdown and return after a page refresh while the app remains open. Allow Auto-Approval is an optional per-project setting for reversible file edits and detected tests plus exact configured build/check commands. Deletions, moves, commits and other unrestricted commands still ask. File changes are backed up; commands can affect your PC and should be trusted.

Why does Windows SmartScreen show a warning?

The development executable is not code-signed yet, so Windows can show a publisher or reputation warning. Signing and distribution are still being prepared. Public downloads will open at launch; use only the official download link announced here.

How do updates work?

The development build checks this website once a day for a newer version and opens the official download page when an update is announced. Nothing is installed automatically. Public release downloads are coming soon.

Can I use it on more than one PC?

Yes. One purchase lets you, as one person, install and use Offline Coder on any computers you own or use. Each person needs their own copy. It is portable: unzip it anywhere, even on a USB drive.

Can the assistant send emails on its own?

No. Gmail and advanced IMAP/SMTP connections can search, read, summarize and prepare drafts; the Outlook extension currently reads mail only. Sending requires your Send button and confirmation of the recipients, subject and body. Project Auto-Approval does not authorize email sending. External emails are treated as untrusted content.

How do extensions connect?

The development build includes twelve connection adapters and sixteen local instruction packs, grouped by specialty. Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar use browser-based Google sign-in after the app's Google registration is configured. GitHub, GitLab, Notion, Slack, Dropbox, OneDrive and Outlook currently use service access tokens with the required permissions. Tokens stay encrypted on this PC. Live account validation and Google verification remain release requirements; instruction packs work offline. Local Ghidra inspection requires separately installed Ghidra/GhidraMCP. Local MCP connects to one reviewed local HTTP server, with approval for every tool invocation even in Auto-Approval projects.

Are the specialist roles separate expert models?

No. The 82 roles guide your selected local model using focused instructions. Auto chooses a role locally, without an extra routing model call. A specialist consultation makes one additional model call with the supplied context. Responses depend on the model and information available; advanced or practical work may need current references or qualified review.

When can I download Offline Coder?

Offline Coder is coming soon. This website previews the development build; public downloads and the final price will be announced at launch. Optional service connections still need live account validation and applicable Google verification before release.

Can I save, archive or delete chats?

The development build saves chats on your PC, separately by project and for general conversations. Titles are generated locally from the chat topic and can be renamed. New chat keeps earlier conversations. Search titles, reopen, archive and restore from Chats. Permanent deletion asks for confirmation. Conversations do not sync to a cloud account.

Are settings grouped into tabs?

Yes. Assistant, Models & speed, Knowledge, Extensions, Connections, Appearance and Updates each have their own category tab. Switching categories preserves input values, and shortcuts open the relevant category.

Do specialists use the built-in tools automatically?

The built-in search, outline, syntax, Python lint/type and OCR tools are available when project tools are on, which is the default for opened projects. Specialists are instructed to choose relevant tools without another toggle. Knowledge imports build local meaning search automatically in the background. Model tool choice and diagnostics can still need review. Optional service connections need setup; every Local MCP invocation requires approval, including in Auto-Approval projects.

Does image OCR need an online service?

No. The development build bundles an English OCR engine and recognition data. Read project images or import an image into Knowledge without an online OCR account or Windows language-pack setup. PNG, JPEG, BMP and TIFF are supported; only the first frame of a multi-page image is read. Recognition can be wrong, so verify important text.
